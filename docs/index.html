Adaptive Learning Bandit

A tutor that picks each student's next maths question with a multi-armed bandit, compared with a fixed textbook sequence. The tutor sees only right and wrong answers; it has to infer what the student is ready for.

Simulated students. Every result here comes from a simulator with stated assumptions about how people learn. It shows how the policies behave under those assumptions, not how real students would respond.

Share of questions on each topic, in blocks of 20. The textbook moves on by the clock; the bandit moves on when the student does.

Does the result survive different assumptions?

Bandit minus textbook, final mastery, when one simulator assumption is changed. Bars are 95% intervals; settings were tuned once on the baseline and not re-tuned.

Mastery runs from 0 to 1 and is the average across six topics. Bandit design follows Clement et al. (2015). Method and limits: see the repository README.
